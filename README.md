# Supply Chain Threat Analysis: SolarWinds Orion Compromise

In-depth research analyzing the SolarWinds Orion supply chain attack, discovered in December 2020 — one of the most impactful supply chain attacks to date. The report examines the incident from its background and scope to a detailed technical breakdown of the attack stages, mapped to the MITRE ATT&CK framework, along with a documented incident timeline and an analysis of detection failures and mitigation measures.

## Contents

- `SolarWinds-Threat-Analysis.pdf` — Full research report

## Report Structure

- **Part A — Overview:** Background, key organizations involved, scope and impact of the attack, based on documented and credible sources
- **Part B — Technical Analysis:** Detailed breakdown of each attack stage, including techniques used, impact, and mapping to the MITRE ATT&CK framework, along with a full documented incident timeline
- **Part C — Root Cause & Mitigation:** Analysis of why the attack went undetected for months, and security measures that could have prevented or enabled earlier detection of the breach
